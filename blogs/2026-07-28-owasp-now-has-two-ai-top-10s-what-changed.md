In 2023, when OWASP introduced the Top 10 for LLM Applications, it was exactly what the industry needed. Suddenly we had a common language for AI risk. Prompt injection Sensitive information disclosure Insecure output handling Training data poisoning Overreliance on model output These weren’t abstract fears anymore — they were categorized, named, and made actionable.
